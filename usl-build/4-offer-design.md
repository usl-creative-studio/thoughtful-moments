## 1. INPUTS CARRIED FORWARD FROM STAGES 1 TO 3

**Problem statement:** Busy, high-earning men in committed relationships struggle to make their partner feel genuinely loved and prioritised because their careers consume the time, energy and planning capacity that intentional acts of service require, and the default fallback (spending money on a gift or a night out) does not read as effort to the partner.
**Persona:** Men 35 to 55, $250k+, committed relationships, time-hungry careers. This stage cuts it further to an occasion inside 60 days in Dallas, TX (Section 3).
**UVP:** A complete at-home experience he delivers with his own hands; everything except the gesture is done for him.
**MVP core features:** flagship at-home massage night: consultation, food via partner, flowers and card, setup team, cue cards, his massage, collection and cleanup. Tea and dinner available on request. No inventory before the experiment passes.
**Price hypothesis from Stage 3 (preliminary):** $2,500 founding rate, $3,500 standard. Floor about $2,100 after labour; no price below $2,000 without a changed format. **Kept at $2,500 / $3,500 at Step 10**, with the finding that anchored parts (~$1,285) do not support the price; the price rests on visible authorship and is what the experiment tests.
**Revenue-model hypothesis from Stage 3:** one-off per-night sale, deposit plus balance. **Validated as the primary thesis** at Step 8; occasion-year prepay recorded as runner-up for the follow-up call.
**Desirability rubric from Stage 1:** Pull. He reaches for the identity of the attentive partner and her reaction.
**Caps applied:** none.
**Cap status, affected dimension, and resolution condition:** none.

## 3. NICHE AND AVATAR

**Locked niche:** Men aged 35 to 55 in banking, consulting, medicine, enterprise sales or founder roles, living in or near Dallas, TX, in a committed relationship of five years or more, with her birthday or an anniversary inside the next 60 days, who can host a private evening at home.

**Demand state:** voluntary category spend (night out, jewellery, done-for-you spa at $600 to $1,500) [researched for the category; belief for this persona]; occasion-driven repeat [category seasonality, researched]. Nothing observed for this persona yet.
**Desirability orientation:** Pull. Signals to look for: voluntary spend on her, occasion behaviour, return for the next occasion, referral inside his circle.
**Founder fit, recorded separately:** both founders are in or adjacent to these careers in Dallas; the founder has lived the problem; two people can deliver five nights a month. No proof or testimonials yet.

**Emotional register:** Approach-motivated (reaching for the identity), with an avoidance undertone (fear of drift). Founder's constraint: "genuine". No hype, no manufactured scarcity.

## 4. OFFER MODEL, CONVERSATION, AND FUNNEL ARCHITECTURE

**Highest-value next conversation:** a Dallas man with an occasion inside 60 days, on the consultation call after a deposit. It must surface the occasion, what he last did for her and how it landed, and whether he will give the massage himself. It informs the price decision and the effort thesis.

**Offer theses considered:**

| Thesis | What is paid for | Payer | Unit | Why it won or lost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Conventional control: one-off night, deposit plus balance | Visible authorship of an act of care, delivered with certainty | He | One night | Won for the test: cleanest evidence on the one unknown, fully reversible |
| B. Occasion year: her birthday, the anniversary, Valentine's, prepaid | Never missing an occasion again; intentionality as a pattern | He | Three nights | Runner-up: fits the drift problem best; prepay behaviour unshown; offered at follow-up after night one |
| C. Firm-paid retention perk for senior staff | The firm's care for its people | Employer | Cohort | Parked: payer unknown, long cycle; revisit after 20 nights |
| D. Done-for-you-plus: therapist gives the massage, he does card, plating, flowers | The experience | He | One night | Stage 2 fail fallback at $1,500 to $2,000; not the product |
| E. Free cue-card guide as lead magnet, paid setup layer | The setup | He | One night | Rejected for now: reach is not the test's constraint and it invites the $297 DIY-box comparison |

**Selected primary thesis:** A, the one-off night.
**Credible runner-up:** B, the occasion year, tested on the follow-up call.

**Conversation offer:** "Tell us about her": a short form (occasion date, city, three things she loves, one thing she has said about him lately) that returns a one-page plan for the night with the price. Accepting costs five honest answers.

**Signal ladder:** Curiosity: page read to price. Qualification: form completed with a date inside 60 days and Dallas. Commitment: deposit paid. Purchase: balance paid after the call.

**Ads target:** niche A only.
**Landing:** the flagship page; noindex during the 30-day test.
**The ask:** the deposit (primary) or the form (secondary).
**After capture:** consultation call within 48 hours; night two to three weeks later.
**Instrumentation list:** visitors by source, scroll to price, form completions, deposits with warm/cold tag, call outcomes and objections heard, hours per night, her reaction at follow-up, referral or rebook.
**Capacity ceiling:** 5 nights and about 10 consultation calls a month. This caps ad spend.

## 5. VALUE EQUATION AUDIT

| Term | Available? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Dream outcome | Yes, as intent | "The look on her face." No relationship-outcome or therapeutic claims |
| Perceived likelihood | Capped | No track record or testimonials. Substitution: certainty about what he will do (call, run sheet, cue cards, practice video), not about how she will feel |
| Time delay | Partly | Bottleneck is sourcing (two weeks) and getting her out for setup. Only claim: one 20-minute call and the night is planned |
| Effort and sacrifice | Deliberately not zero | His one job is the massage; everything else is removed. The effort is the product |

**Capped terms and why:** perceived likelihood, structural (no proof yet). Outcome claims, category (no therapeutic or relationship promises).
**Substitutions used:** certainty of execution in place of certainty of outcome.
**The bottleneck:** sourcing lead time and the setup window while she is out. Any speed claim attaches to the call, not the night.

## 6. THE OFFER

**Working title:** the at-home massage night (done-for-you setup). Provisional.
**Dream outcome:** "The look on her face when she realises I did this."
**What the customer is really paying for:** visible authorship of an act of care, with certainty of execution. Coordination, curation and discretion are the delivery, not the purchase.
**Beneficiary:** she receives the night; he receives her reaction and the identity.
**Payer:** he does, alone.
**Unit of sale and payment trigger:** one night; $500 deposit on the page holds a slot and books the call (refundable until the date is confirmed); balance before sourcing, about two weeks out.
**Core:** the at-home massage night.
**Included components:** consultation and night plan; setup team and room transformation; food for two via partner; bouquet and his card; massage cue cards and practice video; conversation cards; premium oils, candles, linens; collection and cleanup; discretion protocol.
**Genuine additional bonuses:** None.
**Risk reversal / guarantee:** delivery commitment plus refundable deposit. "Your deposit is fully refundable until we confirm your date on the call. On the night, if the room, the food or the flowers are not what we agreed, you do not pay for the night."
**Scarcity:** five nights a month, because two people set up and collect every one.
**Urgency:** her date, with a two-week booking cutoff; founding rate for the first five nights.
**Price:** $2,500 founding rate; $3,500 standard. One-off.

## 10. BRAND INPUTS (FOR Stage 5)

**Who we are for:** men in Dallas with her date coming, who want her to feel loved and have no hours to make it happen.
**The problem we solve:** "I am never around. She says I am not present. Everything I buy her is a function of my wealth, not my care."
**What makes us different:** everything except the gesture is done for him; the gesture is his. Effort that is visibly his, with certainty he can deliver it.

**Register constraint:** genuine. No hype, no manufactured scarcity, no promises about her reaction, no therapeutic language. Approach-motivated with an avoidance undertone: name the drift softly, never shame him.
**Brands or businesses the user admires:** not captured this session.
**Tone direction:** emotional first; the founder's rule is "trust the feeling over everything else."
