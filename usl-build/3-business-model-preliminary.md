## 3. ECONOMIC ARCHITECTURE

**Primary archetype:** capacity-constrained service, with an inventory-bearing physical component (consumables per night, reusable capital items after pass).
**Secondary streams:** none in the test. Possible later: standard rate step-up, second and third experiences, occasion-driven repeat, subscription (deferred with trigger in Stage 2).
**Economic unit:** one delivered night.
**Beneficiary:** the partner receives the night; the man receives her reaction and the identity of having done it.
**Payer, funder, or investment owner:** the man.
**Money or authorization event:** $500 deposit on the page (reserves the slot), balance due once the consultation confirms the date and before sourcing begins.
**Delivery unit:** one night: consultation, sourcing, setup, his delivery, collection and cleanup.
**Governing constraint:** founder delivery capacity (evenings and mornings for two people) and, in the test, deposit conversion. Volume is capped at 5 a month by choice.
**Sales or approval motion:** self-serve deposit from the page, then a 20-minute consultation call. Expected cycle: days, not weeks; occasion-driven, so bookings cluster around anniversaries, birthdays, Valentine's.

**Formula set and calculations:**

Contribution per night (before founder labour) = price - fees - variable cost
- Founding rate, mid cost: 2,500 - 75 - 700 = **$1,725** (69%)
- Founding rate, high cost: 2,500 - 75 - 985 = **$1,440** (58%)
- Standard rate, mid cost: 3,500 - 105 - 700 = **$2,695** (77%)

Contribution per night after shadow-priced founder labour ($600):
- Founding, mid: **$1,125**. Founding, high: **$840**. Standard, mid: **$2,095**.

Monthly contribution capacity (before labour) = nights x contribution
- 5 nights at founding, mid cost: **$8,625 per month**
- 5 nights at standard, mid cost: **$13,475 per month**

Break-even on cash actually at risk before a deposit exists (~$1,500 paid reach): one night at any modelled case. Break-even on the full $10k if all of it were spent (ads plus capital plus insurance): 6 nights at founding mid cost, 4 at standard.

Cash timing: deposit covers the bakery order and flowers; balance before sourcing covers the rest. Each night is cash-positive before delivery. No inventory cash before pass.

| Output | Downside | Base | Upside |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Scenario | 2 nights/month at $2,500, high cost | 5 nights/month at $2,500, mid cost | 5 nights/month at $3,500, mid cost |
| Contribution per night (before labour) | $1,440 | $1,725 | $2,695 |
| Contribution margin | 58% | 69% | 77% |
| Monthly contribution (before labour) | $2,880 | $8,625 | $13,475 |
| Monthly contribution (after $600/night labour) | $1,680 | $5,625 | $10,475 |
| Break-even on $1,500 test spend | 2 nights | 1 night | 1 night |
| Cash before break-even | ~$1,500 (reach) plus one night's consumables fronted before balance clears | same | same |
| Governing requirement | 2 deposits/month | 5 deposits/month from 100+ qualified visitors (3 to 5% deposit rate) | same, plus no price objection at $3,500 |

**Most sensitive variable:** deposit conversion. At 100 qualified visitors a month, 5 nights needs 5% to deposit; 2 nights needs 2%. Below 1% the model is not broken, it is unreachable at this reach cost, and the lever is channel before price. Second most sensitive: founder hours per night; at 20 hours instead of 12 the after-labour contribution at founding rate falls to ~$725 and 5 a month becomes a second job.
**Structural or testable weakness:** testable. Every modelled unit is positive, including the downside. The weakness is volume, not margin, and the experiment tests it directly.

## 5. PRICE HYPOTHESIS

**Lever and recommendation:** price per night. $2,500 founding rate for the first cohort (up to 5 nights), $3,500 standard rate shown on the page from day one. *Revised 2026-09-11 (founder decision, context/decisions.md): $1,500 for the first three bookings, $2,500 after; $3,500 dropped. The experiment now tests conversion at $1,500; $2,500 is tested by bookings four and five.*
**Terms:** $500 deposit reserves a slot and the consultation; refundable until the date is confirmed on the call; balance due before sourcing begins (about two weeks before the night); 5 nights a month; one city; includes consultation, food via partner, flowers and card, setup team, cue cards, collection and cleanup; he gives the massage.
**Economic floor:** variable cost $435 to $985 plus $600 shadow labour = $1,035 to $1,585 per night. A 50% margin after labour puts the floor near $2,100. $2,500 clears it; anything under $2,000 does not, which is why the fail decision in Stage 2 changes the format (done-for-you-plus) rather than cutting price further.
**Market reference:** done-for-you in-home couples massage $599 to $1,499 (two operators); massage plus chef plus florals $1,499 (one operator); DIY romantic setup $297 (one operator). Limitation: a handful of operators in three US cities; no national survey.
**Value or willingness ceiling:** the founder's anchor of $2,500+ on a night out for her (user belief, unsourced). Mintel 2026 supports premium ladders for affluent gifters but gives no price point for this category. No observed spend from the persona yet.
**Confidence:** Low. The floor is solid; the ceiling rests on belief.
**What would move it:** 3 of 5 deposits with 2 cold in 30 days confirms $2,500 and opens the $3,500 test. Zero deposits at 100+ qualified visitors moves the format, not the price.

Stage 4 may revise the package against this floor and these limitations. It may not price below $2,000 without a changed format.

## 6. FIRST CHANNEL TEST

**Target, payer, and decision maker:** men matching the persona in the launch city; he decides alone.
**Channel or adoption path:** primary: direct personal outreach from both founders to men in their own professional circles (the persona's careers overlap the founders' networks), with an explicit ask to forward to one other man. Secondary: a capped paid social campaign in the launch city using interest and behaviour proxies (platforms no longer target on income). Instagram presence supports credibility but is not the channel.
**Access advantage:** the founders are in or adjacent to the demographic; warm reach is free and fast. Limitation: warm buyers may buy out of goodwill, so every deposit is tagged warm or cold and the pass requires 2 cold.
**Proposition:** everything she reads as effort, without the hours you do not have. You give the massage; we do the rest. $2,500 founding rate, 5 nights a month.
**Sequence and expected ranges:** 40 to 80 warm messages and 1 forward each → 100 to 150 page visits warm; paid: ~$1,500 → 300 to 1,000 visits depending on CPC → 100+ qualified visitors combined → 5 to 10 consultation requests → 3 to 5 deposits. Ranges are reasoned, not benchmarked.
**Time and cash cap:** 30 days; $1,500 paid; founder time about 10 hours on outreach.
**Primary signal:** deposit paid.
**Success threshold:** 3 of 5 slots with deposits, at least 2 cold.
**Failure threshold:** 0 deposits at 100+ qualified visitors.
**Inconclusive condition:** 1 to 2 deposits, or under 100 qualified visitors (channel result: change channel, keep offer).
**Evidence capture:** tagged links per source; page analytics; deposit ledger with warm/cold tag; consultation notes.
**Decision table:** as recorded in `2-solution-mvp.md` Section 5A.

## 9. RISKS AND DECISION

| Priority | Risk or unknown | Consequence | Leading indicator and threshold | Mitigation or test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Deposit conversion below 1% at $2,500 | Model unreachable at this reach cost | Under 1 deposit per 100 qualified visitors | The experiment; fail decision changes format before price |
| 2 | Persona not reachable for $1,500 | Test never runs | Under 100 qualified visitors in 30 days | Warm network first; inconclusive-on-visitors rule changes channel |
| 3 | Founder hours per night run 20+ | After-labour contribution halves; burnout at 5 a month | Time log over 12 hours on two consecutive nights | Tighten the setup kit; drop the 100 roses for a bouquet; cap at 3 until process is fixed |
| 4 | Food partner cost or reliability | Night one missing its food element; margin squeezed | No signed per-order price two weeks before the first night | Get two quotes before the page goes live |
| 5 | Warm goodwill inflates the pass | Capital bought on a false signal | Fewer than 2 cold deposits | Warm/cold tag; pass requires 2 cold |

**Decision reasoning:** the unit is profitable in every case modelled, including the downside, and the cash at risk before a deposit exists is about $1,500. What is missing is not margin but evidence that anyone converts at $2,500. That is a testable unknown with a capped test already designed. Test before committing; do not buy capital items or build beyond the page until the day-30 result.
