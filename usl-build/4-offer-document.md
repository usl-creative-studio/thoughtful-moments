## THE OFFER IN ONE PARAGRAPH

For a man in Dallas with her birthday or an anniversary coming and no hours to plan anything, this is one night at home that she will experience as entirely his. One 20-minute call plans it. A two-person team transforms the room, brings the food and the flowers, leaves before she arrives, and comes back the next morning to clear everything away. He gives her the massage himself, with numbered cue cards and a practice video the day before, and the card is in his words. $2,500 for the first five nights (standard rate $3,500), $500 refundable deposit to hold one of five slots a month. If the room, the food or the flowers are not what was agreed on the call, he does not pay for the night.

---

## WHO THIS IS FOR

**Niche:** Men aged 35 to 55 in banking, consulting, medicine, enterprise sales or founder roles, in or near Dallas, TX, in a committed relationship of five years or more, with her birthday or an anniversary inside the next 60 days, who can host a private evening at home.

**Their central motive, in one sentence:**
> He wants her to experience him as the man who made the effort, and he has no way to make that true in the hours he actually has.

**Prior behavior:** dinners out, jewellery, the trip, flowers, the $2,500 night out with drinks nobody wanted. Money spent; effort not perceived. [Reasoned, from the founder's own experience; not yet observed in buyers.]

**Explicitly not for:** single men; anyone who cannot host privately at home; a relationship in crisis; men who want a professional to give the massage; anyone who wants a box shipped.

## WHY THIS CROWD, NOW

**Desirability orientation:** Pull. He reaches for the identity of the attentive partner and her reaction. The drift he already feels is the undertone, and the copy leads with that feeling; the ask is the reaching.

**Demand state:** voluntary category spend on her (nights out, jewellery, done-for-you in-home spa at $600 to $1,500); occasion-driven repeat around birthdays, anniversaries, Valentine's. Experiences are now the default premium gift (57% of US gifters, Mintel 2026), so "an experience" is no longer scarce; effort that is visibly his is.

**Strongest behavioral evidence:** Reasoned; not yet observed. Category comparables have paying customers at $600 to $1,500 done-for-you (Honor Concierge, Bee Yee Fruitful, 2026-09-11). No one has yet paid for a DIY-delivered night at this price.

**Trigger:** her date, inside 60 days, with a two-week sourcing cutoff.

**Founder fit, recorded separately:** both founders are in or adjacent to these careers in Dallas; the founder has lived the problem; two people can deliver five nights a month. No proof or testimonials yet.

## THE PROMISE

**Dream outcome:** "The look on her face when she realises I did this."

**What we can honestly claim:** everything except the massage is done for him; one call plans the night; the team is gone before she arrives and back the next morning; he will know exactly what to do, minute by minute. We do not claim anything about her reaction, the relationship, or any therapeutic benefit. Certainty is offered about what he will do, not about how she will feel.

**Where the real delay sits:** sourcing (two weeks) and getting her out of the house for setup. The only speed claim: one 20-minute call and the night is fully planned.

---

## THE COMMERCIAL MODEL

**What the customer is really paying for:** visible authorship of an act of care, with certainty of execution. Coordination, curation and discretion are how it is delivered; they are not what he is buying.

**Beneficiary:** she receives the night; he receives her reaction and the identity of having done it.
**Payer:** he does, alone.
**Unit of sale:** one night.
**Payment timing:** $500 deposit on the page holds the slot and books the call; refundable until the date is confirmed on the call; balance due before sourcing begins, about two weeks before the night.

**Why this model beat the conventional version:** it is the conventional version. The one-off night won the model lab because it produces the cleanest evidence about the single unknown (will he pay for authorship) and is fully reversible. The occasion-year prepay (three nights: her birthday, the anniversary, Valentine's) is the runner-up and is offered at follow-up after night one, not on the page.

**Economic conditions:** variable cost $435 to $985 per night excluding founder labour; about 12 founder hours per night; 5 nights and about 10 calls a month; deposit conversion of 3 to 5% from 100+ qualified visitors; food-partner quote confirmed before the first night.

## RISK REVERSAL

**Risk-reversal type:** Access or delivery commitment, plus a refundable deposit.

**Exact wording:** "Your deposit is fully refundable until we confirm your date on the call. On the night, if the room, the food or the flowers are not what we agreed, you do not pay for the night."

**Why this type:** Pull router. Stand behind delivery, not her reaction. No outcome guarantee: her feelings are not ours to promise and an avoidance-tinged buyer reads outcome guarantees as hype.

**Requires professional review:** Yes. The delivery commitment wording alongside the massage waiver and contraindication card, and the deposit terms.

## THE CONVERSATION OFFER

**Standalone value offered:** "Tell us about her": a short form that returns a one-page plan for the night, with the price.
**Who it is for:** the man; he is the payer.
**What accepting requires:** five honest answers: occasion date, city, three things she loves, one thing she has said about him lately, whether he will give the massage himself.
**What the conversation must reveal:** the occasion, what he last did for her and how it landed, whether he will do the massage, and any of the six objections in his own words.
**Curiosity signal:** page read to price.
**Qualification signal:** form completed with a date inside 60 days and Dallas.
**Commitment signal:** deposit paid.
**Purchase signal:** balance paid after the call.
**Decision rule:** as recorded in Stage 2: 3 of 5 deposits with 2 cold in 30 days keeps the one-off night at $2,500 and opens the $3,500 test; 0 deposits at 100+ qualified visitors moves to done-for-you-plus before any price cut; 1 to 2 deposits, or under 100 visitors, follows the Stage 2 inconclusive rules.

## LAUNCH SEQUENCE

**Offer conversation test before full launch:** this is the Stage 2 experiment. Primary: the one-off night at $2,500. Runner-up: the occasion-year prepay, offered only on the follow-up call after night one. Audience: founders' Dallas networks with a forward-to-one-man ask, then capped paid social. Invitation: "Her date is coming. Give her one night she will know was yours. You give the massage; we do everything else." Commitment: the $500 deposit. Capture: the instrumentation list. Decision rule: above.

**1. Ships now, zero build:**
The page and deposit link; the "Tell us about her" form; the consultation script; the warm outreach messages; the refund terms.

**2. Cheap builds, high value:**
Massage cue cards (a day); the five-minute practice video shot on a phone (half a day); the reveal note and card template (hours); the run sheet template (hours); contraindication card and waiver draft for review (a day); follow-up script and next-occasion reminder (hours).

**3. Real builds:**
The repeat-appearing driver first: his confidence to deliver, which is the cue cards plus video plus consultation rehearsal as one rehearsed sequence. Then the Dallas bakery or caterer partnership. Then, after a pass, the setup kit (table, linens, candles) and the follow-up afternoon tea as an included second moment.

**4. Blocked on an unknown:**
Insurance quote (general liability). Waiver and delivery-commitment wording (professional review). Food-partner per-order price (two quotes).
