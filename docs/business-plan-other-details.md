# Mission and Vision

The mission of DIY Gifting is to provide a unique and memorable experience for wealthy men and their partners, by offering them a range of DIY gifting options that enhance their intentionality, connection, and intimacy. The vision of DIY Gifting is to become the leading provider of DIY gifting services in the US, and to expand to other markets where there is a demand for high-end and customized gifts.

# Viability Analysis

The viability analysis of DIY Gifting is based on the following assumptions and projections:

- The target market is wealthy men who make above \$300,000 per year and who are looking for ways to improve their relationships with their partners.

- The service is offered online, through a user-friendly and secure website, where customers can choose from three segments: afternoon tea and specialty baked goods, DIY massaging service, and DIY dinner and cue cards for conversations.

- The service is delivered through a subscription model, where customers pay a monthly fee of \$500 and receive four DIY gifting kits per month, one for each week. The kits contain all the necessary ingredients, tools, instructions, and tips for creating the gifts.

- The service is supported by a team of experts, who provide guidance, feedback, and suggestions to the customers, through phone, email, or chat.

- The service is differentiated by its high quality, exclusivity, personalization, and convenience.

- The service is expected to generate revenue of \$6 million in the first year, based on 1,000 subscribers, and to grow by 20% annually, reaching \$14.4 million in the fifth year.

- The service is expected to incur costs of \$3.6 million in the first year, including fixed costs of \$1.2 million (website development and maintenance, marketing, salaries, rent, utilities, etc.) and variable costs of \$2.4 million (ingredients, tools, packaging, shipping, etc.). The costs are expected to increase by 10% annually, reaching \$5.9 million in the fifth year.

- The service is expected to achieve a break-even point in the second year, and to generate a profit of \$2.4 million in the fifth year, with a profit margin of 16.7%.

# Products and Services

DIY Gifting offers three segments of products and services, each designed to create a different and memorable experience for the customers and their partners. The segments are:

- Afternoon tea and specialty baked goods: This segment includes a kit that contains various types of tea, such as black, green, herbal, or fruit, and different kinds of specialty baked goods, such as scones, muffins, cookies, or cupcakes. The kit also includes a tea infuser, a teapot, a tea cozy, a tea tray, a tea towel, and a set of tea cups and saucers. The kit comes with instructions on how to brew the perfect cup of tea, how to decorate the baked goods, and how to set up a cozy and romantic tea party.

- DIY massaging service: This segment includes a kit that contains various types of massage oils, such as lavender, rose, jasmine, or sandalwood, and different kinds of massage tools, such as a massage roller, a massage ball, a massage brush, or a massage glove. The kit also includes a massage mat, a massage pillow, a massage blanket, and a set of massage candles and incense. The kit comes with instructions on how to prepare the massage area, how to apply the massage oils, and how to perform different massage techniques.

- DIY dinner and cue cards for conversations: This segment includes a kit that contains various types of ingredients, such as pasta, rice, chicken, beef, or seafood, and different kinds of sauces, spices, and herbs. The kit also includes a cooking utensil, a cooking apron, a cooking glove, and a set of plates and cutlery. The kit comes with instructions on how to cook a delicious and easy dinner, and how to plate and present it. The kit also includes a set of cue cards that contain questions and topics for stimulating and meaningful conversations.

Each segment is designed to provide a fun and engaging activity for the customers and their partners, that allows them to express their creativity, learn new skills, and bond over a shared experience. Each segment also aims to enhance the intentionality, connection, and intimacy of the customers and their partners, by making them feel special, appreciated, and loved.
